# Rocket Lab Corporation (NASDAQ: RKLB): First Research Report

**As of:** September 3, 2026  
**Primary source:** Rocket Lab Corporation, Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025, filed February 26, 2026.

## What Rocket Lab does

Rocket Lab is an end-to-end space company. It sells launch services and Space Systems products and services, including spacecraft design, manufacturing, components, mission operations, and optical systems. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 43]

Its two reportable businesses are Launch Services and Space Systems. Electron is its small orbital launch vehicle; the company is also developing the medium-capacity Neutron vehicle. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 43–44]

## What improved

Revenue rose 38% to $601.8 million in 2025 from $436.2 million in 2024. Management attributed the increase to $91.9 million of growth in organic Space Systems products and services and $73.7 million from higher launch cadence. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 45, 47]

Electron launch cadence rose from 16 missions in 2024 to 21 in 2025. Revenue per launch increased from $7.8 million to $8.5 million, while cost per launch declined from $5.7 million to $4.8 million; management attributes the cost decline to scale efficiencies. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 45]

Backlog increased to $1.8473 billion at year-end 2025 from $1.0670 billion a year earlier. Of the 2025 total, $1.3717 billion related to Space Systems and $475.6 million to Launch Services. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 45]

## What remains difficult

Rocket Lab reported a 2025 operating loss of $228.8 million and a net loss of $198.2 million, even as gross margin improved to 34.4% from 26.6% in 2024. Research and development expense increased 55% to $270.7 million, primarily because of Neutron development, hiring, and prototype spending. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 46–48]

Backlog is not guaranteed future revenue. Rocket Lab states that launch-services and spacecraft-build contracts typically include customer termination rights, and long-term contract margins can change if costs to complete rise or delivery schedules slip. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 45]

Neutron is central to Rocket Lab's growth opportunity, but management says its first launch was then targeted for Q4 2026 and acknowledges that development, manufacturing, testing, and infrastructure risks could cause further delays. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 44]

## What the earlier filing says about execution

In the 2024 Form 10-K, management targeted Neutron's first launch for the second half of 2025, while noting that the schedule could slip. The 2025 Form 10-K later targeted Q4 2026. This shift does not prove Neutron will fail, but it is concrete evidence that its timeline is an execution risk I need to monitor. [2024 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 43; 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 44]

The 2024 report also shows why the profit question matters: Rocket Lab said it had not achieved profitability and expected continued operating-expense and capital-expenditure growth. [2024 Form 10-K, Risk Factors, p. 17]

## More recent company-reported developments

Rocket Lab reported Q2 2026 revenue of $234.1 million, up 62% year over year, and backlog of $2.36 billion. The same release reported a GAAP operating loss of $57.5 million for the quarter, so the latest growth did not yet equal GAAP profitability. [Rocket Lab Q2 2026 financial-results release, August 10, 2026]

On June 29, 2026, Rocket Lab presented its proposed acquisition of Iridium Communications. The investor update states that the deal implies an approximately $8 billion enterprise value for Iridium, with $3.6 billion of committed debt financing; closing is expected in mid-2027, subject to stockholder and regulatory approvals. This is a potential strategic expansion, but it also adds transaction, financing, and integration risk. [Rocket Lab Investor Update, June 29, 2026, pp. 10–11]

I am interested in Rocket Lab because it already has operating businesses in launch and space systems, growing revenue, and a substantial backlog. I also think the company has meaningful long-term potential if it can turn this growth into durable profitability. **UNSOURCED — my judgment.**
